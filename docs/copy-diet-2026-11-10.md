# Gameplay copy diet — 2026-11-10

Owner direction: "Make the game less copy intense. It is OK for the BRD and
report pages to give the user all the details. But simplify the gameplay."

Copy only. No mechanics, prices, budgets, scoring, simulation, audio, or
layout structure changed. The BRD overlay, the report, and the field guide
(MPOR) keep every section and every word; gameplay screens now point to
them instead of repeating them.

## Measurement

(`innerText` words): title screen, HUD, Level 1 setup (all four stages),
the run screen at a fixed moment (copy-bearing elements only; sprite

### Why not 40%

The brief set a 40% target with zero decision-critical facts lost. After
this pass, the remaining gameplay text is almost entirely content the
brief itself protects:

- Question texts: dealt cards (~192 words), bank rows (~130), QC cards
  (~95). These are the game content, not explanation.
- Data rows a choice is made from: segment stat rows (~200), card and
  bank metadata (~180), invite sender/subject/lines (~100).
- Prices, thresholds, and state readouts: budget panel, launch bar,
  checklist states, HUD, run counters (~450).

Reaching 40% would require deleting question text, prices, or segment
data, or a layout change (for example collapsing the question bank or
the checklist by default). Both are outside this brief; either is a
separate decision for the owner.

## Decision-critical lines deliberately kept

- Validation catch-all caveat ("a small share always survives: catch-all
  domains cannot be verified") — the tier decision depends on it.
- Reminder costs in the label (8 cr, -5 goodwill per wave) and the
  second-wave prices on the SECOND PUSH card.
- Suppression skip penalty (-6 goodwill) in the toggle text.
- Every price on buttons, toggles, cards, and the stakeholder choices;
  every win threshold (response targets, quality 60+, Insight 60+/70+,
  goodwill 35+ on Level 4, patience minutes).
- Segment stat rows (size, timing, relevance, history, list health,
  send cost) and the persona mix percentages — the audience decision is
  made from them.
- The instrument help's A-F section key — the section tags on every
  card have no other key; removing it would strand a first-time player.
- The Quick Challenge banner's AGENDA line — in QC mode the agenda
  panel is hidden, so the banner is the only agenda display.
- The checklist's non-blocking disclaimer ("INFORMATION ONLY · THE
  BUTTON ENFORCES BUDGET + MINIMUM") — a standing novice-UX
  requirement, shortened, not dropped.
- Carried audience-history counts in the readout (see above).
- Respondent voice lines and the report's manager captions — flavor and
  report content, out of scope.
